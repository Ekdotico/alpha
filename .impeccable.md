# Ekdotico alpha site — Design Context

**The house style lives in `@ekdotico/domico/.impeccable.md`. Read it first; it
governs.** This file holds only what is true of this repository and of no other.

## What is specific to this repository

**The surface.** One public landing page at `/`, plus `/privacy` and
`/confirmed`. A copy of deree's landing page without its gate, its preview
banner, or its research material, with a waitlist form in the hero and in a
closing section above the footer.

**The reader.** Someone from the academic-integrity community — faculty,
integrity officers, researchers — who has met Ekdotico through that community
and opened the link once, often on a phone. They have not used the product and
are deciding whether to hear more.

**The job to be done.** Say what Ekdotico is and why the provenance record
matters, then let the reader leave an address. **The page explains, then
offers; it does not press.** One call to action, the same form twice. No
urgency, no counts, no social proof. Seriousness and rigour, never a sales page.

**What follows from that.** The hero is deree's fluid display heading, with the
form under the lede so the offer is on the first screen. Everything else —
surfaces, rules, radii, colour, motion, the 72-character measure — is the
package's, unmodified.
